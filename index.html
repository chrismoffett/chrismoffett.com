Chris Moffett is a philosopher and educator who studies how people learn through movement and attention. He is a Research Scholar at the Digital Futures Institute, Teachers College, Columbia University.

In 2008 he co-founded Aesthetic Relational Exercises, and he is now writing Animacy. His CV lists his publications and teaching. To get in touch, write to him.
