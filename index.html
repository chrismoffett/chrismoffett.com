Chris Moffett

Pressing into the earth in various configurations over and over. Occasionally making vibrations in the air or rearranging other pressing things.

Most of the pressing happens where philosophy, education and the body push back on one another: at the Digital Futures Institute at Teachers College, Columbia University, where I am a Research Scholar; in essays and chapters gathered on Google Scholar and Academia.edu and set out at length in my CV; as approximately a third of Aesthetic Relational Exercises; and now in Animacy, written for experienced somatic practitioners whose practice, however fluent, has gone flat.

If something here presses on your own work, write to me, or find me on Instagram.
