Chris Moffett

Pressing into the earth in various configurations over and over. Occasionally making vibrations in the air or rearranging other pressing things.

Most of the pressing happens where philosophy, education and the body push back on one another. I am a Research Scholar at the Digital Futures Institute at Teachers College, Columbia University, where I teach courses on technical culture and embodied learning. The essays and chapters are gathered on Google Scholar and Academia.edu, and the long version is in my CV. Approximately a third of Aesthetic Relational Exercises. Now writing Animacy, for experienced somatic practitioners who find that their practice, however fluent, has gone flat.

If something here presses on your own work, write to me. I am also on Instagram.
